# Dasha Timing Skill

## Vimshottari Dasha System Overview
The Vimshottari Dasha is the foundational predictive timing system in Vedic astrology (Parashari system), mapped over a 120-year human lifespan. It unfolds in a fixed nine-planet sequence based on the Moon's nakshatra (lunar mansion) at birth:

1. **Ketu**: 7 years (Introspection, spiritual detachment, unexpected shifts)
2. **Venus (Shukra)**: 20 years (Creativity, relationships, material comfort, arts)
3. **Sun (Surya)**: 6 years (Authority, career visibility, vital energy, father figures)
4. **Moon (Chandra)**: 10 years (Emotional well-being, public connection, motherhood, travel)
5. **Mars (Mangal)**: 7 years (Ambition, courage, initiative, property, conflict management)
6. **Rahu**: 18 years (Ambition, foreign endeavors, technology, illusion, sudden gains)
7. **Jupiter (Guru)**: 16 years (Wisdom, higher education, progeny, expansion, dharma)
8. **Saturn (Shani)**: 19 years (Discipline, perseverance, structural growth, karmic lessons)
9. **Mercury (Budha)**: 17 years (Intellect, commerce, communication, analytical pursuits)

Total: 120 years.

## Hierarchy of Periods
- **Mahadasha (MD)**: Major ruling period shaping the overarching life chapter.
- **Antardasha / Bhukti (AD)**: Sub-period activating specific life themes under the Mahadasha lord's umbrella.
- **Pratyantardasha (PD)**: Micro-timing period refining events to weeks or days.

## Analytical Workflow
1. **Identify the Operating Periods**:
   - Determine current Mahadasha and Antardasha lords from the birth nakshatra and elapsed balance.
2. **Examine Planetary Dignity & Functional Nature**:
   - Identify whether the period ruler is a functional benefic (Lagnesha, 5th/9th Trikona lord, Yogakaraka) or functional malefic (6th, 8th, 12th Dusthana lords, 2nd/7th Maraka lords) for the specific Ascendant/Lagna.
   - Note planetary strength: exaltation (uccha), moolatrikona, own sign (swakshetra), debilitation (neecha), or combustion (asta).
3. **Assess Planetary Relationship (Sambandha)**:
   - Determine the angular relationship between MD lord and AD lord.
   - Harmonious angles (1/5/9 trines, 1/4/7/10 kendras, 3/11 upachayas) indicate growth, support, and constructive action.
   - Challenging angles (6/8 Shadashtak or 2/12 Dwirdwadashtak) indicate friction, health adjustments, or unexpected transitions.
4. **Transit Alignment (Gochar)**:
   - Check transits of slow-moving planets (Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu/Ketu) relative to the natal Moon and Lagna.
   - Double-transit principle: Jupiter's and Saturn's mutual blessing on a house triggers significant life events during an active Dasha of related house lords.
5. **Timing Windows & Advice**:
   - **Favorable Windows**: Auspicious periods for starting ventures, weddings, investments, or career expansion.
   - **Cautionary Windows**: Periods calling for prudence, restraint, risk minimization, and targeted remedies.
