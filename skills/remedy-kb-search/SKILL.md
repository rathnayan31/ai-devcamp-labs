# Grounded Astrological Remedies Skill

## Philosophy of Vedic Upayas (Remedies)
In Vedic astrology (Jyotish), remedies (upayas) are designed to harmonize planetary energies, balance karmic imbalances, and strengthen positive potential. They fall into three classical gunas:
- **Sattvic (Pure / Spiritual)**: Mantras, meditation, seva (selfless service), daan (charitable giving), fasting (vrat), pilgrimages, and ethical conduct. Recommended universally.
- **Rajasic (Active / Material)**: Gemstones (ratna), yantras, rituals, pujas, and yagyas. Require strict diagnostic assessment.
- **Tamasic (Extreme)**: Forbidden; avoid any superstitious or fear-motivated practices.

## Safety Guidelines & Precautions
1. **Gemstone Safety Rules**:
   - Never recommend a gemstone simply based on a weak or afflicted planet. Wearing a stone amplifies that planet's vibrations.
   - If a planet is a **functional malefic** (e.g., lord of 6th, 8th, or 12th houses, or Maraka lord of 2nd/7th) for the user's Lagna, wearing its gemstone can intensify illness, accidents, or losses.
   - Prioritize stones for **functional benefics** (Lagnesha, 5th lord, 9th lord, Yogakaraka) that are weakly placed.
   - Always state that gemstone recommendations require verification by a qualified practitioner.
2. **Universal Safe Remedies**:
   - **Mantra Chanting**: Japa of Vedic Beej Mantras, Gayatri mantras, or Navagraha Stotras carries no adverse physical counter-indications.
   - **Charitable Giving (Daan)**: Giving away items associated with an afflicted planet on its planetary day is universally beneficial and harmless.
   - **Behavioral Adjustments**: Lifestyle upayas (e.g., honoring elders for Jupiter, respecting laborers and maintaining discipline for Saturn, cleanliness for Venus).
3. **Grounding & Policy Constraints**:
   - Ground all recommendations in classical treatises (*Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra*, *Phaladeepika*, *Saravali*).
   - Strict read-only advisory role: Never prompt users to pay for rituals, purchase talismans, or succumb to fear or fatalism.
